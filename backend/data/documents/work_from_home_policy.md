# Work From Home (WFH) Policy — EnWIDTH Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2025  
**Document Owner:** Human Resources & Operations

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the guidelines for employees who wish to work from a location other than the official EnWIDTH Technologies office premises. The goal is to support work-life balance while maintaining productivity and collaboration standards.

---

## 2. Eligibility

- All full-time confirmed employees who have completed their **probation period (3 months)** are eligible for Work From Home.
- Employees on probation are required to work from the office unless otherwise approved by HR.
- Contractual and intern staff are not eligible for WFH without special approval.

---

## 3. WFH Days Allowed

- Employees are allowed to work from home for a maximum of **2 days per week**.
- WFH days must be approved by the **direct manager** at least **24 hours in advance**.
- Monday and Friday WFH requests require **additional justification** to prevent extended weekends.
- No more than **3 consecutive WFH days** are allowed without special exception.

---

## 4. How to Request WFH

1. Employee sends a WFH request to their manager via the internal portal or email.
2. The request should specify the date(s) and reason for working from home.
3. Manager approval must be received before the WFH day starts.
4. Retroactive WFH approvals are not permitted except in genuine emergencies.

---

## 5. Responsibilities While Working From Home

Employees working from home are expected to:

- Be available during **standard working hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST)**.
- Attend all scheduled meetings via video call with camera on unless otherwise agreed.
- Maintain productivity equivalent to in-office work.
- Respond to messages and emails within **30 minutes** during working hours.
- Ensure a professional background and quiet environment for video calls.

---

## 6. Equipment and Internet

- Employees are responsible for arranging their own internet connection for WFH.
- The company will **not reimburse** home internet costs unless the employee has a company-sponsored data plan.
- Company-issued laptops must be used for all work tasks. Personal computers should not be used for accessing company systems.
- Employees must use the **company VPN** when accessing internal systems from home.

---

## 7. Data Security During WFH

- Do not work from public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports) without using the company VPN.
- Lock your screen when not at your desk.
- Do not allow family members or others to access your work device.
- Any security incident that occurs during WFH must be reported to IT Security immediately.

---

## 8. Performance Monitoring

- Managers may review WFH productivity at any time.
- If productivity during WFH is found to be below expectations, the manager reserves the right to revoke WFH privileges.
- WFH is a privilege, not a right, and can be suspended at the company's discretion.

---

## 9. Team Meetings and Collaboration

- Team meetings scheduled on WFH days take precedence — employees must attend.
- Employees should be fully accessible via Slack, email, and video during WFH hours.
- If a client visit or urgent office requirement arises, employees must report to the office regardless of WFH approval.

---

## 10. Special Circumstances

- Employees with medical conditions requiring extended WFH may apply for a special accommodation through HR.
- Such requests require a doctor's certificate and HR approval.
