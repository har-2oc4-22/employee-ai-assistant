# Employee Benefits — TechCorp Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 2.2  
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2025  
**Document Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Overview

TechCorp is committed to the well-being of its employees and their families. The following benefits are available to all full-time confirmed employees unless otherwise noted.

---

## 2. Health Insurance

- All confirmed employees and their **immediate family members** (spouse + 2 children) are covered under the company's Group Medical Insurance plan.
- **Sum insured:** ₹5,00,000 per family per year.
- Coverage includes hospitalisation, pre- and post-hospitalisation (30 and 60 days), daycare procedures, and maternity (after 9 months of waiting period).
- **Parents:** Employees may add parents to the policy by paying a subsidised premium of ₹8,000 per year per parent.
- The company does **not** provide pet insurance of any kind.

---

## 3. Life Insurance

- All employees are covered under a Group Term Life Insurance policy.
- Coverage: **3× annual CTC**, up to a maximum of ₹1 crore.
- Coverage is active from the first day of employment.

---

## 4. Accidental Insurance

- Group Personal Accident Insurance covers all employees.
- Benefits include permanent partial/total disability, temporary total disability, and accidental death.
- Coverage: **2× annual CTC**.

---

## 5. Retirement Benefits

### 5.1 Provident Fund (PF)
- Both employee and employer contribute **12% of basic salary** to the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF).
- Employees can withdraw PF balance under specific conditions (housing, medical, education).

### 5.2 Gratuity
- Employees who complete **5 years of continuous service** are entitled to gratuity as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.

---

## 6. Wellness Benefits

- **Gym/Fitness Allowance:** ₹1,500 per month reimbursement for gym membership.
- **Annual Health Check-up:** Full-body health check-up sponsored by the company every year.
- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** Free access to mental health counselling sessions (up to 6 sessions per year, fully confidential).
- **Yoga/Wellness Classes:** Monthly virtual wellness sessions organised by HR.

---

## 7. Learning & Development

- **Learning Allowance:** ₹25,000 per year for approved online courses, certifications, and books.
- **Conference Attendance:** Employees can apply to attend up to 2 conferences per year with company sponsorship (subject to manager approval).
- **Internal Training:** Free access to TechCorp's internal learning platform.

---

## 8. Meal Benefits

- Employees receive **₹2,600 per month** as a meal allowance (tax-exempt up to the applicable limit).
- The Bangalore and Mumbai offices have subsidised cafeterias.

---

## 9. Transport Benefits

- **Cab Facility:** Door-to-door cab pickup and drop is available for employees working late (after 9 PM) or early (before 7 AM).
- **Transport Allowance:** ₹1,600 per month for commuting (tax-exempt up to the applicable limit).

---

## 10. Internet & Phone Allowance

- Employees in roles requiring regular connectivity receive a **phone + internet allowance of ₹1,500 per month**.
- Engineers in the Platform team receive an additional ₹500/month for home internet.

---

## 11. Stock Options (ESOPs)

- Senior engineers, leads, and managers may be eligible for ESOPs as part of their compensation package.
- Vesting schedule: **25% per year over 4 years** with a 1-year cliff.
- ESOP details are specified in the individual employment letter.

---

## 12. Flexible Benefits Plan (FBP)

- Employees can customise their salary structure by choosing components such as:
  - Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)
  - House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  - Special Allowance
- The FBP is reviewed once a year during the appraisal cycle.

---

## 13. Referral Bonus

- Employees who refer a candidate who is subsequently hired and completes **3 months** receive a referral bonus.
- **Junior roles:** ₹25,000
- **Mid-level roles:** ₹50,000
- **Senior/Lead roles:** ₹1,00,000

---
