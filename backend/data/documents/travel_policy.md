# Travel Policy — EnWIDTH Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 1.5  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document Owner:** Finance & Operations

---

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all EnWIDTH Technologies employees travelling on official company business, including client visits, conferences, training, and inter-office travel.

---

## 2. Pre-Approval

- All business travel must be **pre-approved** by the employee's direct manager and the Finance team.
- Approval must be obtained **at least 5 business days** before the travel date for domestic trips.
- For international travel, approval must be obtained **at least 15 business days** in advance.

---

## 3. Domestic Travel Entitlements

### 3.1 Air Travel
- Employees at **Senior Engineer / Analyst level and above** are entitled to economy class air travel.
- Director and above may travel business class on flights over 4 hours.

### 3.2 Train Travel
- Employees may book **AC2 or AC3** tier depending on availability.
- Tatkal bookings are reimbursable only if standard booking was unavailable.

### 3.3 Hotel Accommodation
| City Tier | Allowed Daily Rate |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Metro (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai) | ₹5,000 per night |
| Tier 2 cities | ₹3,000 per night |
| Tier 3 cities | ₹2,000 per night |

### 3.4 Daily Allowance (DA)
- **Metro cities:** ₹800 per day
- **Tier 2 cities:** ₹600 per day
- **Tier 3 cities:** ₹400 per day

---

## 4. International Travel Entitlements

- Economy class for flights under 8 hours.
- Business class is permitted for flights over **8 hours** with Finance approval.
- Hotel accommodation up to **USD 150 per night** or equivalent.
- Daily allowance: **USD 60 per day** for meals and incidentals.

---

## 5. Local Conveyance

- Cab/taxi expenses during business travel are reimbursable.
- Use of personal vehicle requires prior approval; reimbursed at ₹10/km.
- Auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs (Ola, Uber) are reimbursable with receipts.

---

## 6. Expense Claims

- All expenses must be submitted within **7 working days** of returning from travel.
- Submit via the Finance portal with original receipts.
- Expenses submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed.
- The following expenses are **not reimbursable:**
  - Alcohol
  - Personal entertainment (movies, spas, etc.)
  - Mini-bar items
  - Fines and penalties

---

## 7. Travel Insurance

- All employees travelling internationally on official duty are covered under the company's corporate travel insurance.
- Coverage includes medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and loss of baggage.
- Employees must carry their company travel insurance card when travelling internationally.

---

## 8. Advance Payment

- Employees may request a travel advance of up to 80% of estimated expenses.
- Advances must be settled within 7 days of return.

---

*For queries on travel policy, contact finance@enwidth.com.*
