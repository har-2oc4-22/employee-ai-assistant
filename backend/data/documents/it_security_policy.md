# IT & Information Security Policy — EnWIDTH Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document Owner:** IT & Information Security

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes rules and guidelines for the acceptable use of EnWIDTH Technologies's IT resources and the protection of company data and systems.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use of IT Resources

- Company-issued devices (laptops, phones, tablets) are primarily for business use.
- Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not:
  - Consume excessive bandwidth
  - Interfere with work productivity
  - Violate any other policy

---

## 3. Password Policy

- Passwords must be a minimum of **12 characters** long.
- Must contain: uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character.
- Passwords must be changed every **90 days**.
- Password reuse is prohibited for the last **10 passwords**.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT support.
- Use the company-approved password manager (1Password) for storing credentials.

---

## 4. Software Installation

- Only software approved by the IT department may be installed on company devices.
- Employees must not install cracked, pirated, or unlicensed software.
- To request software installation, submit a ticket to IT helpdesk.

---

## 5. Data Classification

| Level | Description | Examples |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| Public | Safe to share externally | Marketing brochures |
| Internal | Internal company information | Policies, org charts |
| Confidential | Sensitive business data | Salary data, financial reports |
| Restricted | Highest sensitivity | Customer PII, source code |

---

## 6. Data Handling

- Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or unapproved cloud storage (Google Drive personal, Dropbox, etc.).
- Use the company's approved file-sharing tools (OneDrive, SharePoint).
- Encrypt Confidential/Restricted data before transmission.

---

## 7. VPN Usage

- Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
- The VPN must be enabled before connecting to any company server or internal application.
- Splitting tunnelling is disabled by default on the company VPN.

---

## 8. Email Security

- Do not open attachments from unknown senders.
- Report phishing emails to security@enwidth.com immediately.
- Do not click suspicious links; use the Phishing Alert button in Outlook.
- EnWIDTH Technologies will **never** ask for your password via email.

---

## 9. Incident Reporting

- All security incidents (lost device, suspicious email, data breach, unauthorised access) must be reported to the IT Security team within **1 hour** of discovery.
- Email: security@enwidth.com | Hotline: +91-1800-TECH-SEC
- Failure to report a security incident is itself a policy violation.

---

## 10. Remote Access

- Remote desktop access to office systems is allowed only via the company-approved VPN.
- Employees must not expose company systems to the internet without IT approval.
- RDP and SSH access to production systems requires dual-factor authentication (2FA).

---

## 11. Clean Desk Policy

- Sensitive documents must not be left on desks unattended.
- Lock your workstation (Windows+L) whenever you leave your desk.
- Paper documents containing Confidential or Restricted data must be shredded before disposal.

---

## 12. Social Engineering

- Never share company information (systems, employees, clients) with unverified external parties.
- Verify the identity of any caller claiming to be from IT before providing any information.

---

## 13. Consequences of Violation

- Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Legal action may be taken in cases of deliberate data theft or sabotage.

---

*For IT support: helpdesk@enwidth.com | For security incidents: security@enwidth.com*
