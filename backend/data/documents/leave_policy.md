# Leave Policy — EnWIDTH Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

**Version:** 3.1  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2025  
**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department

---

## 1. Overview

EnWIDTH Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("the Company") recognises that employees need time away from work for rest, personal matters, and unforeseen circumstances. This policy defines the types of leave available, eligibility criteria, and the process for requesting leave.

---

## 2. Annual Earned Leave (EL)

- Every confirmed employee is entitled to **18 days of Earned Leave (EL)** per calendar year.
- Leave accrues at a rate of **1.5 days per month** starting from the date of confirmation.
- Earned Leave can be **carried forward** up to a maximum of **30 days** to the next calendar year.
- Leave exceeding the carry-forward limit will lapse.
- Employees must apply for EL at least **3 working days in advance** (except for emergencies).

---

## 3. Casual Leave (CL)

- Every employee is entitled to **6 days of Casual Leave** per calendar year.
- CL cannot be carried forward to the next year.
- CL is not encashable.
- A maximum of **3 consecutive days** of Casual Leave may be taken at one time.

---

## 4. Sick Leave (SL)

- Employees are entitled to **10 days of Sick Leave** per calendar year.
- Medical certificate is required for Sick Leave of **more than 2 consecutive days**.
- Sick Leave cannot be carried forward.
- Sick Leave cannot be encashed.

---

## 5. Maternity Leave

- Female employees who have completed **6 months of service** are entitled to **26 weeks of Maternity Leave** as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Maternity Leave is fully paid.
- Application must be submitted at least **4 weeks** before the expected date of delivery.

---

## 6. Paternity Leave

- Male employees who have completed **6 months of service** are entitled to **10 days of Paternity Leave**.
- Must be taken within **6 months** of the child's birth.

---

## 7. Bereavement Leave

- Employees are entitled to **3 days of Bereavement Leave** for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion.

---

## 8. Leave Without Pay (LWP)

- Leave Without Pay may be granted when all paid leaves are exhausted.
- LWP requires approval from the Department Head and HR.
- Excessive LWP may affect performance ratings and increments.

---

## 9. Applying for Leave

1. Employees must submit a leave request via the Employee Portal or through their manager.
2. The request must include: start date, end date, leave type, and reason.
3. Approval is at the manager's discretion.
4. Emergency leave can be applied retroactively with a valid explanation.

---

## 10. Public Holidays

- EnWIDTH Technologies observes **12 national public holidays** per year.
- A list of public holidays is published at the beginning of each year by HR.
- If a project requires working on a public holiday, a compensatory leave is granted.

---

## 11. Leave Encashment

- Unused Earned Leave up to **15 days** can be encashed at the time of exit.
- Sick Leave and Casual Leave are not encashable.

---

## 12. Misuse of Leave

- Misuse of leave (e.g., working for another employer while on leave) is a disciplinary offence.
- False medical certificates will result in immediate disciplinary action.

---

*This policy is subject to revision. Employees will be notified of any changes.*
